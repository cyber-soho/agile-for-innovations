How to play

  1. Pick a mission on the map. Missions 1–4 follow the four lessons of Class 10; Mission 5 is Lab 05; the Vault holds every link, video and abbreviation.
  2. Seven stages per mission: ① a real problem to reason about · ② the lesson with its slide numbers · ③ the slides with speaker notes · ④ a build challenge · ⑤ six flash-cards · ⑥ six formative quiz questions · ⑦ explain the idea back in one sentence.
  3. XP and levels: every stage gives experience points (XP); finishing all seven gives a badge. Levels: Observer → Interviewer → Framer → Maker → Tester → Design Thinker.
  4. Why problem first? The game teaches “to the problem, not to the tools” — the principle Elon Musk has described for the school he set up for his children: start from a real problem, reduce it to what is certainly true, then discover the method you need.
  5. Progress stays in this browser only. Nothing is graded; nothing is sent anywhere.
