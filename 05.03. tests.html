Before you start

The Scrum Guide 2020 (scrumguides.org) requires a Product Backlog and its refinement — a description, an order and a size — but it does not prescribe user stories, story points, velocity or burndowns. Those are common practices with named authors (Cohn, Jeffries, Wake, North, Grenning, the DSDM community). When two answers look plausible, choose the one that follows the lecture's rules: a named user, a benefit that differs from the goal, at most one ✗ per story, an observable then, points relative to a reference, partly finished = 0, round up.
