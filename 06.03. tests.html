Before you start

Two free guides define the words of this class: the Kanban Guide (Coleman & Vacanti, edition 2025.5) gives WIP (Work In Progress), throughput, work item age, cycle time, the Definition of Workflow and the SLE (Service Level Expectation); the Kanban University guide to the Kanban Method gives the six general practices, the commitment and delivery points and the word lead time. The Scrum Guide 2020 remains the reference for everything Scrum says. When two answers look plausible, choose the one that follows the lecture's rules: WIP = started and not finished; a duration = the later date minus the earlier date; throughput counts items, not points; average cycle time = average WIP ÷ throughput; a limit is a signal to talk, not a quota.
