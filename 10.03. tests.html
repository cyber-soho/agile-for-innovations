How to use this hub

  • ✅ Flash-cards fix the vocabulary — the “what”. Click a card to turn it; filter by lesson.
  • ✅ Self-check trains judgment — the “why”. Each MCQ (Multiple-Choice Question) has 4 options and one right answer. Each MSQ (Multiple-Select Question) has 6 options; the number of right answers is shown in round brackets, e.g. (3 right answers).
  • ✅ Every question has a hint and, after you answer, an explanation for every option — read the wrong ones too.
  • ✅ At the end you get your score and a breakdown per lesson, so you know which lesson to re-read.

Before you start

The method has no single owner: this hub follows the five modes of the Stanford d.school Bootleg, the Design Council's Double Diamond and the usability research of the NN/g (Nielsen Norman Group).
